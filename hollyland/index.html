02 / 05
Who we are

Two divisions.
One roof.

KSM Production makes films, documentaries and commercials. KSM Media House handles social campaigns, reels and brand content. In practice it means the same team that shoots a feature can turn around a vertical campaign the following week, without outsourcing anything.

We're not a single creator with a camera. Around ten to fifteen people work with us regularly, and we cover the whole chain in-house — concept and script through direction, shooting and post. Most of us have known each other for years, which is the main reason we can move fast on set.

05 / 05
What we're offering

Not influencer content.

This autumn we go into two short films and two documentaries back to back, with the features continuing in parallel — a lot of real shooting days in varied conditions: interiors, location, night, handheld and rigged. Anything we put on those sets gets tested properly, and it ends up on camera: unboxings, hands-on reviews, behind-the-scenes from actual film sets rather than staged demos, plus the vertical cutdowns our Media House team produces every week.

It's a working production house using the gear the way your customers will, and documenting it honestly. If something doesn't work, we'd tell you first, privately — that's the only way this stays useful to both sides.

Happy to jump on a call any time, and I can send over our collaboration deck with visuals if that helps.
